Products rarely die because of the code.

After four years building and running ENS Vision, we saw the same pattern again and again. Great teams lose because nobody owns the business side.

  • A launch with no go-to-market plan

    The product ships, the announcement goes out, and nothing happens. No segment, no channel, no first buyers lined up.

  • A great product nobody hears about

    Features keep growing while reach stays flat. The story only makes sense to the people who built it.

  • The wrong structure, discovered too late

    The company, the token or the licence sits in the wrong place for the users, the bank or the next round.

  • No bank willing to open an account

    Crypto and fintech teams lose months to banking and payment partners that were never going to say yes.

  • Partner deals signed without reading the fine print

    Exclusivity, revenue shares and listing terms that look good in a call and hurt for years.

That's true in web3, and just as true for AI and SaaS. It's the part of the company we run.

Questions founders ask us

What does a go-to-market sprint include?

A fixed-price sprint of 2 to 4 weeks. We start with a basics check, then sharpen who you sell to and why they'd switch, build a go-to-market plan for one segment and one channel, and open the first conversations with real buyers and partners.

How much does it cost?

Every sprint has a fixed scope and a fixed price, agreed after a short intro call. If it works, we continue month to month. No long contracts.

Do you only work with crypto projects?

No. We work with startups across web3, AI and SaaS. What they share: a strong product and a business side that is thin or missing.

Can you help us set up in Malta, the EU or offshore?

Yes. We map the options for company setup, licensing (including MiCA), tax and banking, then coordinate licensed lawyers and accountants and keep the process moving. We don't give legal advice ourselves.

Do you do security audits?

We run a light security and compliance check before launch: dependencies, TLS, GDPR and cookies, basic smart-contract checks. Full audits and penetration tests go to specialist partners we trust.

Where are you based?

Dot Vision Ltd is a company registered in Malta, in the EU, with its office in Valletta. We work remotely with teams worldwide.
