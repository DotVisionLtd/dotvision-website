Questions founders ask us

What does a go-to-market sprint include?

2 to 4 weeks, fixed price. Basics check, who you sell to, one segment and one channel, first real conversations.

How much does it cost?

Fixed scope and price, agreed after a short intro call. Then month to month if it works.

Do you only work with crypto projects?

No. Web3, AI and SaaS teams that are building, with a thin business side.

Can you help us set up in Malta, the EU or offshore?

Yes. We map setup, licensing (including MiCA), tax and banking, then work start to end with our legal partner MK Fintech Partners (Malta) and licensed accountants. We don't practice law ourselves.

Can you prepare our MiCA white paper?

Yes, we've delivered one. We write it with your team, our legal partner MK Fintech Partners (Malta) reviews it, and it is lodged with an EU regulator before you offer the token or list it. EU exchanges delist tokens without one.

Do you do security audits?

A light check before launch. Full audits and pentests go to specialist partners.

Can you build the product too?

Our engineering partner Schatzl IT Solutions in Graz, Austria builds web3 and web2 products, from smart contracts to apps and backends, plus technical reviews before you raise. If you already have developers, our partner can add extra hands or a second opinion.

Where are you based?

Valletta, Malta (EU). We work remotely, worldwide.
